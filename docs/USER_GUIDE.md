# AI Shopping Assistant - User Guide

Welcome to the AI Shopping Assistant! This guide will help you understand how to use the shopping assistant to find the perfect outfits for your travels.

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Open the application in your browser
2. Enter your Customer ID (e.g., "CUST-0000001")
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Login" to access the shopping assistant

### Starting a Conversation

Once logged in, you'll see the chat interface. The AI assistant will greet you and is ready to help you find clothes for your travels.

---

## How to Use the Shopping Assistant

### 1. Tell the Assistant About Your Trip

Start by sharing your travel plans. The assistant understands natural language, so you can say things like:

- "I'm traveling to Paris next week"
- "Planning a beach vacation to Miami in February"
- "Business trip to London for 5 days"
- "Going hiking in Colorado this weekend"

### 2. Answer Clarifying Questions

The assistant may ask follow-up questions to better understand your needs:

- **Destination**: Where are you going?
- **Travel Dates**: When are you traveling?
- **Activities**: What will you be doing? (sightseeing, business meetings, hiking, etc.)
- **Preferences**: Any specific style, color, or brand preferences?

### 3. Use Suggestion Bubbles

After each response, you'll see **suggestion bubbles** - clickable buttons with helpful responses. These suggestions are tailored to your conversation:

- If you haven't mentioned a destination, you'll see destination suggestions
- If dates are missing, you'll see time frame options
- If activities aren't specified, you'll see activity suggestions
- After seeing products, you'll see options to explore more

Simply click a suggestion bubble to send it as your next message!

### 4. Browse Product Recommendations

When the assistant has enough information, it will show you product recommendations:

- **Product Cards**: Each product shows an image, brand, name, price, and available sizes
- **Hover for Details**: Hover over a product to see available sizes
- **Add to Cart**: Click "Cart" to add items to your shopping cart
- **Quick Buy**: Click "Buy" for instant purchase

### 5. Shopping Modes

The assistant supports two shopping modes:

#### Online Mode
- Standard e-commerce experience
- Add to cart and checkout online
- Products delivered to your address

#### In-Store Mode
- Shows store location information ("Floor 1")
- Shows real-time stock availability
- "Show me" button displays store map with product location

## Features

### Context Popup

When discussing a travel destination, a **context popup** appears showing:
- Destination name
- Travel dates
- Trip duration
- Current weather at the destination

This helps you see at a glance what the assistant knows about your trip.

### Product Details

Click on any product card to open the **detailed view**:
- Larger product image
- Full description
- Size options
- Color variations
- Customer ratings
- Add to cart functionality

### Shopping Cart

Access your cart by clicking the cart icon:
- View all added items
- Adjust quantities
- Remove items
- See total price
- Proceed to checkout

### Agent Thinking (Optional Debug View)

The system tracks how the AI processes your requests internally. This information includes:
- Which agents are working on your query
- What information has been extracted
- Decision-making process

This data is available in the API response for debugging purposes.

## Tips for Best Results

### Be Specific
The more details you provide, the better recommendations you'll get:
- ✅ "I need formal wear for business meetings in Tokyo next month"
- ❌ "I need clothes"

### Mention Activities
Activities help the assistant recommend appropriate clothing:
- Hiking → Outdoor gear, hiking boots
- Business → Formal wear, dress shoes
- Beach → Swimwear, light fabrics
- Sightseeing → Comfortable walking shoes, layers

### Specify Preferences
If you have preferences, share them:
- "I prefer blue colors"
- "Looking for Nike or Adidas brands"
- "My budget is around $200"
- "I wear size Medium"

### Start Fresh
If you want to plan a completely different trip, say:
- "Let's start over"
- "New trip"
- "I want to plan a different vacation"

## Common Questions

### How does the assistant know the weather?
The assistant automatically fetches current weather data for your destination to recommend weather-appropriate clothing.

### Can I see more product options?
Yes! Just ask:
- "Show me more options"
- "Any other styles?"
- "What about different colors?"

### How do I change my trip details?
Simply update the assistant with new information:
- "Actually, I'm going to Rome instead"
- "Change the dates to next month"
- "Add hiking to my activities"

### Is my conversation saved?
Yes, your conversation is saved so you can continue where you left off. To start fresh, use the reset option or say "start a new trip."

## Troubleshooting

### Products Not Loading
- Refresh the page
- Check your internet connection
- Try resetting the conversation

### Assistant Not Responding
- Wait a moment - AI responses may take a few seconds
- Refresh the page if stuck
- Check if you're logged in

### Incorrect Recommendations
- Provide more specific details about your needs
- Correct any misunderstandings ("I meant hiking, not biking")
- Reset and start with clearer information
